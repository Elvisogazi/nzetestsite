About PickWise

A simple way to discover useful products and buy them from the original seller.

Our mission

There are millions of products online and it's hard to know where to start. PickWise highlights a small selection of products we think are worth a look, with clear descriptions and key features so you can decide quickly.

How it works

  1. Browse
    Search or filter our product list by category.
  2. Review
    Open a product to see its full description and features.
  3. Buy
    Click Buy Now and complete your purchase on the seller's own website.

We don't sell anything ourselves. There is no cart, no checkout and no payment on this site. All purchases happen on the seller's website.

Affiliate Disclosure

PickWise is an affiliate website. This means we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through the links on this site. The links on our Buy Now buttons are affiliate links.

Using these links never costs you anything extra, and the price you pay is the same as if you visited the seller directly. The commissions help us keep the site running. We aim to describe products honestly and accurately.

Privacy notice

Before you continue to a seller, we ask for your name, email and (optionally) phone number. We use these details to send you information about the product you viewed and related recommendations. We do not sell your details. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us.
